How to read pricing model

  • Per-1K-text-units / per-1K-text-records: pay-per-call. Linear with traffic.
  • Per-million-tokens: also pay-per-call, denominated in tokens (~4 chars each).
  • Flat-monthly: subscription. Usually with a usage cap.
  • Quote-only: no public price. Procurement-led, contract review required.
  • Open-source-free: zero license cost. FTE and infra remain on the buyer.
