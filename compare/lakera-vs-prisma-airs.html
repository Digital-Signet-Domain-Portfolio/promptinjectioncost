Lakera (Lakera Guard, Lakera Red) vs Palo Alto Networks Prisma AIRS (incl. Protect AI): Cost and Coverage

Side-by-side comparison across price, pricing model, OWASP LLM Top 10 coverage, deployment and headquarters. Verified 2026-06-19.

Headline verdict

Lakera wins on price transparency and time-to-first-deploy: a free Community tier covers 10,000 requests per month with no contract. Prisma AIRS wins on platform reach, since it inherits Protect AI’s model scanner and red-team tooling and bundles inside the wider Palo Alto deal. Pick Lakera if you want a runtime guardrail today. Pick Prisma AIRS if you are already a Palo Alto customer or need scanner, red team and runtime under one contract.

Switching cost

If you are on one and considering the other

Lakera-to-Prisma migrations are dominated by procurement: budget several months of legal review for a Palo Alto enterprise agreement. Prisma-to-Lakera migrations are technical, not contractual: rewrite policy as code against Lakera Guard’s Python and REST SDKs.
