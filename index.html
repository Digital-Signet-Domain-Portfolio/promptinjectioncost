Why this site exists

Every vendor in the LLM-security category, with three hyperscaler exceptions, hides its price behind a contact-sales gate. Buyers cannot build a board paper from contact-sales pages. The independent answer is to put the public prices that do exist on one page, label the rest as quote-only without inventing a number, and add the surrounding total-cost-of-ownership lines (red team, eval tooling, FTE, on-call) that no individual vendor page will admit to.
