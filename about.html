Independence statement

promptinjectioncost.com has no commercial relationship with any vendor on the site. We do not accept payment for placement, ranking, or coverage decisions. We do not run affiliate links. We do not sell the underlying vendor data. The whole site exists to make procurement easier for buyers who refuse to plan a board paper from a contact-sales page.
